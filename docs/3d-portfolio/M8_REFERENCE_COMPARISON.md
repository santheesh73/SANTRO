## 2. Exhibition Quality Bar

1. **Integrated vs Superimposed**:
   - Web cards in typical 3D portfolios float awkwardly in world space with ungrounded CSS transforms.
   - In SANTRO M8, every exhibit has an engineered physical foundation: a travertine plinth, recessed toe-kick reveal, anodized aluminum frame, and PBR surface response.
2. **Authentic Scale**:
   - Exhibits obey architectural scale: featured plinths measure $2.4\text{m}$, standard plinths $1.7\text{m}$, steles $1.15\text{m}$ to $2.1\text{m}$, matching the $3.4\text{m}$ corridor ceiling and $6.8\text{m}$ atrium volume.
3. **Lighting Harmony**:
   - Displays do not project unphysical omnidirectional glow. They interact naturally with M5 directional sunlight and warm interior coves.
