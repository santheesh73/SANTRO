## 1. Architectural Overview & Volumetric Silhouette

## 3. Building Volumes & Massing Breakdown

### 3.1 Substructure & Foundation Plinth
- **Material:** Cast-in-place board-formed concrete and smooth off-white cementitious plinth cladding.
- **Topography Integration:** Anchored into the descending hillside slope. The foundation steps down on the south-west corner, revealing a 2.5m exposed concrete retaining wall beneath the pool terrace.
- **Terrace Deck:** Honed travertine slabs ($1.20\text{m} \times 0.60\text{m}$ grid) laid with $3\text{mm}$ architectural joints.

### 3.2 Ground Floor Volume
- **Recessed Facade Plane:** Sits 3.8m to 4.2m recessed beneath the upper cantilever overhangs, creating deep natural shade.
- **Living Lounge (West Wing):** Fully glazed perimeter with multi-panel sliding pocket glass doors opening directly onto the pool deck.
- **Entrance Portal (Central Axis):** Framed by two $0.60\text{m} \times 0.60\text{m}$ plastered structural columns. Contains the oversized walnut pivot door flanked by $0.70\text{m}$ vertical clear glass sidelites.
- **Dining / Conference (East Wing):** Features a 90° frameless structural glass corner wall providing unobstructed views across the terrace and hillside.

### 3.3 Upper Floor Cantilever Boxes
- **Left Cantilever (West Volume):** 
  - Massive hollow-box aesthetic with $0.40\text{m}$ perimeter wall thickness.
  - Deep $1.50\text{m}$ recessed terrace fronted by an ultra-clear laminated glass balustrade ($1.10\text{m}$ height).
  - Interior houses an upper creative studio / guest suite visible through sliding glass partitions.
- **Right Cantilever (East Volume):**
  - Solid front plaster panel on the left, transitioning into a deeply inset covered terrace on the right.
  - Accent wall of rich horizontal walnut paneling lining the recessed terrace alcove.
- **Central Upper Bay:** 
  - Glazed bridge recessed $2.4\text{m}$ back from the front edges of the flanking boxes, visually separating the two solid masses.

### 3.4 Roof Structure & Bulkhead
- **Parapet:** $0.45\text{m}$ perimeter parapet concealing flat roof drainage and waterproofing.
- **Surface Finish:** Light gray washed river pebble / gravel ballast.
- **Skylights:** Rectangular flush-mounted glass skylight array ($6.0\text{m} \times 1.8\text{m}$) illuminating the central atrium core with natural zenithal light.
- **Stair Bulkhead:** Rectangular geometric penthouse on the rear-east corner housing roof terrace access.

## 4. Architectural Details & Fenestration

### 4.1 Entrance Pivot Door System
- **Leaf Dimensions:** $1.80\text{m}$ width $\times$ $3.20\text{m}$ height $\times$ $100\text{mm}$ thickness (`REFERENCE ESTIMATE`).
- **Pivot Hardware:** Heavy-duty concealed floor and header pivot located $250\text{mm}$ from the left frame jamb.
- **Door Face:** 9 horizontal tongue-and-groove American Walnut planks with hairline shadow gaps.
- **Pull Handle:** Custom $2.10\text{m}$ vertical blackened steel bar handle with an integrated concealed cyan LED channel illuminating the recessed grip.

### 4.2 Architectural Glazing & Frames
- **Exterior Fenestration:** Thermally broken dark charcoal anodized aluminum frames with ultra-slim $32\text{mm}$ sightlines.
- **Interior Partitions:** $12\text{mm}$ frameless low-iron tempered safety glass set into recessed floor and ceiling aluminum U-channels.
- **Glass Balustrades:** $17.5\text{mm}$ laminated structural glass with continuous flush-mounted base shoe profiles and zero vertical mullions.

### 4.3 Interior Gallery Corridor & Timber Wall
- **Feature Wall:** Spans the entire right side of the entrance corridor ($14.0\text{m}$ length $\times$ $3.40\text{m}$ height).
- **Surface:** Vertical fluted walnut battens ($40\text{mm}$ width $\times$ $20\text{mm}$ depth $\times$ $15\text{mm}$ spacing).
- **Mounted Signage:** Flush-cut satin-white architectural lettering spelling out *"THE PORTFOLIO HOUSE"* with room indices below.
- **Ceiling Detail:** $100\text{mm}$ recessed black architectural reveal channel running parallel to the wall, housing micro-aperture square LED downlights.

### 4.4 Floating Minimalist Staircase
- **Structure:** Cantilevered steel stringer concealed within the corridor wall.
- **Treads:** Solid honed limestone stone slabs ($1.20\text{m}$ width $\times$ $0.30\text{m}$ tread $\times$ $0.16\text{m}$ riser) floating with no visible risers.
- **Guardrail:** Minimalist glass panel or open architectural reveal.

### Space 01 — Exterior Approach & Pool Terrace
- Direct dialogue between crisp architectural geometry and rugged natural topography.
- Reflective water surface mirrors the sky and white cantilevers.

### Space 02 — Entrance Foyer & Circulation Spine
- Immediate sensory transition from bright outdoor sunlight into refined, controlled architectural tranquility.
- Warm timber wall contrasts against cool limestone flooring and crisp white plaster.

### Space 03 — Glass Engineering Workspace
- Located immediately to the left of the corridor at midpoint.
- Symbolizes the engineer's creative lab: physical desk with iMac monitors surrounded by high-tech holographic schematics on glass.

### Space 04 — Double-Height Central Exhibition Atrium
- The volumetric climax of the building: expansive ceiling, mezzanine galleries, and central plinths.
- Functions as the primary exhibition space for Santheesh's marquee engineering projects.

### Space 05 — Rear Mountain Vista Terrace
- Glazed terminal point of the interior axis, opening out to panoramic mountain horizons.
