## 1. Exhibition Purpose

The Archive serves as tangible proof of execution. It houses verified hackathon victories, national competition achievements, and open-source contributions. 

Instead of an interactive web accordion or generic timeline slider, records are physically manifested as documentary bronze and travertine tablets mounted along the East under-mezzanine wing of the double-height atrium.
