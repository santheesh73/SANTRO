## 1. Exhibition Purpose

The Contact Pavilion serves as the culmination of the interior spatial journey. It transforms the physical resolution of the house into an invitation for dialogue.

Rather than presenting a web contact form inside a 3D building, the room utilizes the panoramic double-height rear glass curtain wall and natural mountain twilight as an architectural backdrop for direct communication channels.
